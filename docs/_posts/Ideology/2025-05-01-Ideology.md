My Baseline Ideology: On Paper
(As of May 2025)

Wills, ideas, memes. These are the most important things we as humans are solely capable of creating, propagating and enacting. Every other creature acts removed from them entirely or subsumed into a singular one (must eat, must breed, must survive). We as humans are unique and special in both our ability to do this and the ones that we do create inherently are valuable in their own right (I wish to learn, I wish to care, etc). This is circular, but so are all philosophies at their premise. 

From there, everything else is secondary, even life itself. Nature granted chemistry, to biology, to information, to memes. What comes next is beyond me, but I imagine it will be even more beautiful. 

But there is an issue: Wills can come and go, pushed and pulled. Consumed and reused. While this process is natural and good, the individual loses their will oftentimes to the more strong, more dominating, more ubiquitous ones. 

This is done through only two means: Soft and Hard Power. 

Some value one (form of power) over the other in significant ways. Many consider soft power a more reasonable approach to enacting change or propagating wills. After all, how bad could something consented to truly be?

'If they have my best interest at heart, and they pose a good point, I suppose it's only reasonable to agree'.

Others value hard power.

'Folk can be unreasonable, unable to see the bigger picture, they must be made to capitulate or there will be chaos'.

If you are convinced one cannot see reason, it is only rational to oppose their views without any words. Ideologues only speak of one language, ultimately: violence.


I have been consulted about both, and find both evil. Why? The actions taken to enact one's will is inherently oppressive. Without full knowledge of the consequences of one's belief, you cannot in full good faith suggest that the shifting of one's will shall be towards another's favor. It is also not inherent that because consensus is wrought, that the idea is more reasonable, or more right or more moral. Without knowing the consequences of one's actions and words, how can one fully condone trampling or persuading away others?

If science safety led to millions of lives lost due to regulatory bloat, if veganism led to untold animal abuse by nature itself, if democracy led rise to populism, or authoritarianism led to the stifling of progress, or if freedom led to the deaths of children, or if artificial meat led to the long-term mindset shift we cannot fathom, how can you justify any means of will enactment?

You must accept that you can be evil for doing so. No amount of asking around will prevent that from being true. Every human on earth could inadvertently vote to end all life on earth (let's make nuclear bombs, let's make AI, let's save the world). There was no means of knowing how much harm to the planet fossil fuels would bring. And there is no telling if not using them would have led to future prosperity. But few wills have had such consequences. 

The moral weight of action in the absence of clairvoyance means that you are willing to risk unknowable things, using hard or soft power, to accomplish what you or your groups of people believe will benefit themselves. And each will consumed in its wake is a future lost, both its merits and demerits.

The cost of despair in the now from hard power and the cost of naivete from soft power is trivial to this cost.

Given all of these, I pose the axiom: all that empowers the creation and battlefield of wills is good. All that silences and de-legitimizes them is evil. 

Paradoxically, the battlefield of wills is a call to enactment of will, inherently trampling and evil. However, it is the natural consequence of their creation. So long as people have opposing wills (good) conflict in inevitable (evil). 

The silencing of all wills gates off the battlefield and ideas stagnate: Death of all that gives humans value. So, too, does the all-absorbing will. The one that ‘wins out’ totally and perpetually. Nothing is left but one vision, one ideal. All novelty fades and oppression wins eternally.

We have only bettered our own lives (defined by the ability to consider new memes) through their enactment and consequence. If we ever slow that down, so does our ability to create more, a vicious cycle. We escaped it narrowly but the world wants to drown in it once more. There are many offenders of this, But the worst offender of slowing progress is nature itself. While great, we aren't truth seeking, we are tribalistic, and most importantly: we are comfort seeking. Discomfort is the only thing that allows for the enactment of greater wills, the greatest evils. It is inevitable that many of us will fall into the hedonistic treadmill until the very end of our days. Discomfort is the only means to dissuade this entropy.

Humans are also unequal by nature. Some are born with disabilities, others lacking intellectual capacity or physical strength. Coordination speeds, eyesight, energy levels, mental stability, and sleep duration are all left to chance either through genetic dispositions or upbringing, both out of the individual’s control. Humans can often be thought of as slaves of circumstance first, then of wills. This is an inequality that limits both the generation of memes and their enactments through Will.

My goal is to equalize humanity to allow the lions to thrive, conduct evil through their strength of spirit, not of genes. And to eliminate the weakness of humanity and allow them to embrace what we could be, increasing the propensity and lethality of wills equally. 

In the end, we will take over the universe; each individual will be a god in their own right, dictating morality and purpose in their own images. Each individual will is maximized, both in action and in generation.



Appendix
Soft/Hard Power Explanation
Power is defined as the capacity to enact one's will one way or another. Hard power is of the forceful nature. It preys upon one's desires and bargains you would rather lose one thing over the other.
'Do x or you will suffer'.
Passive or active, threats are utilized via an imbalance of raw power. 


Soft Power are of the democratic nature. You are told what seems convincing, what makes sense to you, acknowledging your desires alongside another's, bargaining for one's consent.
'Do x and you will get what you want'.
Generally done non-coercively, wills are combined or re-contextualized to condone their enactment. 

Will-Limiting Ideals
Concepts like China, Authoritarianism, Liberalism, Oligarchies, etc. While these systems offer their own merits and demerits, they either seek to limit men, make men small, make men subservient, or make men simple. Religion was and has been of particular consequence. 

-
For China, it is of a singular will that is ever-oppressive. Any others are completely subsumed by it or eliminated entirely. Same as Russia.

For Liberalism, concepts thrive, but enactment is limited. Inequality is an evil, and thus so too is striving for inequality, the only means to enact will. Hedonism is encouraged and men lack discomfort.

For Religion, morals are carved into stone and men are forced to obtain enemies of conscience, battling themselves over base instincts that would otherwise embolden them. Eternal memes, with strength of religion often defined by its ability to last without change, without growth. Cold-death to individual will.

For Oligarchies, wills are made large, but competition fades. Non-competition between ideals lead to aristocratic-enforced stagnation of memes. 

For Anarchy, while effective in short runs, quickly fades. Chaotic by nature, it cannot last in nearly any societal context.
